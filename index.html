System Help & User Guide

Sunrise Dental Clinic Management System - Staff Guide

Welcome to the system. Follow these step-by-step instructions to perform daily clinical operations efficiently.

1. System Login

  • Enter your assigned username and password on the login screen.
  • Click the Login button to access the main dashboard. Access levels (Admin or Staff) are automatically applied based on your credentials.

2. Managing Patients

  • Navigate to the Patients section from the sidebar.
  • To register a new patient, fill in the Patient Name, Contact Number, and Address in the form and click Save Patient.
  • View all registered patients in the table below. Admins can delete patient records if necessary.

3. Booking Appointments & Searching

  • Go to the Appointments section.
  • Choose whether the patient is New (fill details directly) or Existing (select from the dropdown list).
  • Select the preferred Dentist and Treatment type, then pick the appointment date and time. Click Book Appointment.
  • Use the Search Bar at the top of the appointments table by entering the specific Appointment ID/Number to quickly view individual appointment details.

4. Calculating and Printing Bills (Invoices)

  • Go to the Invoices section to view scheduled appointments.
  • Click Generate Bill next to the target appointment.
  • The system will automatically compute the total cost (combining the treatment price and fixed dental center charge).
  • Select the payment status (Paid/Pending) and click Save & Print Invoice to open and print the official receipt.

5. Managing Dentists & Treatments

  • View the list of available dentists and treatments via their respective sidebar tabs.
  • Admin Users: Have exclusive permission to add new entries or delete existing records using the respective forms.

6. Exiting the System

  • Click the red Logout button at the bottom of the sidebar to securely clear your session and return to the login screen.
